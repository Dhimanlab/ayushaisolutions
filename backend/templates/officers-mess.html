Officer's Mess Management System

Complete digital solution designed for Defence & CAPF Officers’ Messes — full ration control, billing, guest rooms, attendance and permanent audit-ready records.

Built specifically for Defence & CAPF

This system follows the exact requirements of military and CAPF messes. Every transaction is permanently stored with full audit trail so that records are always ready for Board of Officers, Audit, and higher headquarters inspection.

Complete Feature List

Core Module

1. Member & Personnel Management

  • Living-in / Living-out classification
  • Rank-wise hierarchy & privileges
  • Posting in/out & temporary duty tracking
  • Family / dependent details
  • Role-based access (Mess President, Secretary, Officer, Store Keeper, Members)
Most Critical

2. Ration & Inventory Management

  • Separate ledgers: Dry Ration, Fresh, Perishables, Bar/Liquor
  • Opening → Receipt → Issue → Closing Stock
  • Daily / weekly indent system
  • Low stock alerts & automatic reorder suggestions
  • Wastage, surplus & condemnation register
  • Supplier & local purchase tracking
Daily Operations

3. Messing & Attendance

  • Daily headcount for Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks
  • Guest meal tracking (member guest / mess guest)
  • Extra Messing Charge (EMC) calculation
  • Special / medical diet marking
  • Function & party catering booking
Accommodation

4. Guest Room Management

  • Real-time room availability
  • Booking by members & temporary duty officers
  • Check-in / Check-out with automatic charges
  • Guest register (mandatory for military messes)
  • Priority rules based on seniority / duty
Accounts

5. Billing & Accounts

  • Automatic monthly mess bill generation
  • Separate billing for messing, bar, guest room & functions
  • Credit system & dues tracking
  • Mess Fund / Committee Fund accounting
  • Payment receipts & outstanding reports
Audit Ready

6. Full Permanent Record Storage

  • Every transaction stored permanently
  • Immutable audit trail (who, when, what changed)
  • 7–10 years data retention
  • Locked monthly closings after verification
  • Complete history available for any past period
Official Reports

7. Reports & Export

  • Monthly Ration Account Sheet
  • Stock Ledger (item-wise)
  • Member-wise Billing Summary
  • Consumption & Wastage Analysis
  • Guest Room Occupancy Report
  • One-click Excel / PDF export for Audit & Board of Officers
Security

8. Security & Administration

  • Role-based hierarchical permissions
  • Unit / Mess level data isolation
  • Complete activity logs
  • Secure login with strong password policy
  • Daily automatic backups + manual restore
